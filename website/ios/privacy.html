Privacy policy

Chess Analyzer collects nothing about you and doesn't use the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code that reports anything.

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Games, positions, the engine's analysis, photos and pictures you read, and your settings are processed and kept only on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it) may include them, under Apple's terms.

  • Camera. Used only while you scan a page, take a photo of a board or diagram, or use the live camera. The pictures are read on the device.
  • Photos. The app sees only the photo you pick, through the system's photo picker.
  • Recent imports. The last ten pages, photos and pasted texts you read are kept in the app's own folder (Files › On My iPhone › Chess Analyzer › Imports), so that a reading that went wrong can be looked at again. Delete them there at any time.
  • Files. PGN files you open in the app are read on the device.

What the app sends

Nothing. The links in Settings (the source code and this website) open in Safari only when you tap them.

This website

These pages set no cookies and use no analytics, scripts or outside resources.

Children

Chess Analyzer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: chessgame-analyzer@creatica.org.
