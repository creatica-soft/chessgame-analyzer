Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to chessgame-analyzer@creatica.org. Please say which device you use and, for a reading that went wrong, attach the photo or screenshot.

Games

Opening a game

In Games, tap Paste to paste a game you copied, or + to open a PGN file, type a game, or scan or photograph a printed page. A .pgn file can also be opened in Chess Analyzer from Files, Mail or another app's share sheet. A game opens without analysis; tap Analyze Game.

How moves are marked

Each move is compared with the engine's best move in the same position. A move that loses 0.3 of a pawn or more is an inaccuracy, 1 pawn a mistake, 2 pawns a blunder. The limits, and how long the engine searches each position, are in Settings.

Sharing the analysis

Share Annotated PGN sends the game with the evaluations in comments, symbols on the moves, and the better moves as variations.

Reading printed games

In Games, + › Scan with Camera… finds and straightens the page. Hold it flat and well lit. Two columns, commentary and diagrams on the page are fine: the moves are found by their numbers and checked against the rules. A move the app had to guess is marked for you to check, and the recognised text can be corrected and read again.

Reading diagrams

In Position, tap ⋯ › Read a Diagram: take a photo, scan and crop, or choose a photo or screenshot. Crop to the diagram if the page holds several. Check the side to move and castling (Castling and FEN…) before analysing.

Reading a real board

In Position, tap ⋯ › Read a Real Board: Live Camera… reads the board as you hold the phone still above it for a moment; Take Photo… and Choose Photo… read a photo.

  • Stand on White's side, a little higher than the board, with the whole board in view. From very low, pieces near you hide the ones behind them.
  • Use even light; avoid strong reflections on shiny pieces.
  • Squares the app is unsure of are marked: tap them to correct. If the board was found in the wrong place, Adjust Corners lets you set its corners, and Turn Round turns it if White and Black are swapped.

Results on real boards vary with the set, the board, the light and the angle. Standard Staunton sets read best. If your set reads badly, a photo sent to the address above helps improve it.

The engine

Settings sets how many processor cores the engine uses and the size of its memory. When the device gets warm the engine slows down on purpose to cool it, and carries on.
